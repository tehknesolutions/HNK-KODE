# HNK Mora-Kodin Discovery v0.3

**Status:** DISCOVERY DIRECTION — NOT LEXICAL CANON

## Goal

Redesign the 122 provisional Kodins so the haKodan lexicon becomes more syllabic/moraic, pronounceable, compositional and recognizably HNK.

The five existing canonical lexemes are preserved without retroactive morphology:
`AHNUVA`, `EMANU`, `HAYA`, `HODERU`, `KODAN`.

## Design synthesis

The HNK system may study structural principles from:

- Japanese: moraic/syllabic rhythm and strong CV readability;
- Semitic languages: reusable consonantal/root-pattern families;
- Greek: productive stems and derivational families;
- Esperanto: regular, learnable derivation;
- Enochian: optional phonetic/ritual texture only.

These are references. HNK must not silently borrow sacred/semantic authority from another language.

## Preferred phonotactic direction

```text
V
CV
CV.N
CV.CV
CV.CV.CV
V.CV.CV
```

Avoid unnecessary consonant clusters in frequent Kodins.

## Root + pattern hypothesis

A short HNK abstract root may generate a family through regular vocalic/moraic patterns.

Example only:

```text
K-D   root family
KA-DA
KE-DU
KI-DI
KO-DO
KU-DA
```

No semantic assignment is canonical merely because a pattern is structurally elegant.

## Candidate examples

Current redesign examples are discovery candidates only:

- CREATE: KADENA → candidate direction `KADA`
- WORLD: MAVORA → `MAVU`
- OBJECT: OKVAR → `OKU`
- ENTITY: ENTAV → `ENA`
- AGENT: AGENA may survive because it is already mora-friendly
- ACTION: AKANA may survive
- COMMAND: KOMARA may survive

## Production gate

For each provisional concept generate at least three Mora-Kodin variants and score:

1. mora/syllable regularity;
2. HNK identity;
3. reusable root-family coherence;
4. derivational regularity;
5. phonetic distinctness;
6. collision with existing corpus;
7. HNK40/Mandala structural compatibility;
8. glyph/sigil candidate separability;
9. semantic precision;
10. Creator review.

`DISCOVERY_CANDIDATE != HNK_CANON`.
