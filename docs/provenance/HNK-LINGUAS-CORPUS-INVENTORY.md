## Archaeology rules

1. Do not infer authority from repetition.
2. Do not infer authority from being generated by an assistant.
3. Explicit creator approval/correction outranks assistant proposals inside HNK canon governance.
4. Preserve superseded definitions as `LEGACY` when historically meaningful.
5. Preserve explicit rejection as `REJECTED`.
6. Preserve incompatible unresolved evidence as `CONFLICT`.
7. Preserve missing evidence as `GAP`.
8. Never fill a GAP from general knowledge or model inference.
9. Separate structural validity from linguistic authority.
10. Separate Project membership from HNK-KODE membership.
