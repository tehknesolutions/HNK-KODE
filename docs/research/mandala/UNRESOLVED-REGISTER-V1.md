## Evidence taxonomy for future extraction

Every claim should be typed as one of:

- `observed` — directly visible/measured in locked source;
- `derived` — mathematically derived from observed structure;
- `externalReference` — independently sourced historical/esoteric correspondence;
- `hnkCanonical` — explicit HNK governance decision;
- `candidate` — research proposal awaiting approval;
- `unresolved` — insufficient evidence.

No sound, mystical, linguistic or semantic correspondence should be invented from raster geometry.
