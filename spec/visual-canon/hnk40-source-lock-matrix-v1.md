## Authority boundary

The recovered item-level register is `RESEARCH_REGISTER / NON-CANONICAL` for linguistic semantics. E5 is a structural projection, not a silent replacement of HNK40 Legacy.

Recovered E4 evidence reports 40/40 candidates, PATH/EDGE round-trip, HNKP2 CRC, 58-byte packets and immutable HNKP1 evidence, with semantic assignments = 0 and canonical promotions = 0.

Therefore this source-lock **does recover structural identity/classification**, but it does **not** establish phonemes, meanings, numerological values, visual ordinals, PUA assignments, CRC/transport semantics, lexicographic tie-breaks or word mappings.

## State vocabulary

- `RECOVERED` — directly backed by recovered repository evidence.
- `DIRECT` — E5 path is directly compatible under V4.
- `DERIVED_UNIQUE` — exactly one structural E5 projection selected by V4.
- `DERIVED_AMBIGUOUS` — multiple candidate projections remain; no preferred identity.
- `UNRESOLVED` — evidence does not establish the value.
- `VISUAL_CANDIDATE` — asserted by supplied visual boards only.
- `HOLD_SEMANTICS` — structural evidence exists but semantic/phonological promotion is forbidden.
